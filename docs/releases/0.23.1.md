# task-master 0.23.1

0.23.0 was tagged but never published: its Windows build of the speech libraries failed.
0.23.1 is the same release with the Windows build fixed. What 0.23.0 brings:

## Scribe: recording

- Meetings are recorded on Windows and macOS: your microphone and the computer's sound
  (everyone else on a call), as two tracks kept on this device and never synced. A bar on
  every page shows the recording with live levels; a reminder to tell participants.
- An interrupted recording (crash, power cut) can be kept or discarded; recordings are
  compressed after they stop (about 11 MB an hour per track) and play back on the meeting
  page. Settings → Scribe picks the microphone; Settings → Files lists the audio.
- macOS needs 14.6 or later to record the computer's sound; Linux shows meetings without
  recording.

## Scribe: transcription

- Recordings are transcribed on this device (whisper.cpp, Metal on macOS) and the voices
  on the call told apart (Speaker 1, 2, …; your microphone is "Me"). Nothing is uploaded.
- Settings → Scribe → Transcription downloads a model once (Accurate, 1.6 GB, or Fast,
  0.5 GB, plus 33 MB of speaker models), resumable and checked; pick the language or let
  it be detected; transcribe automatically when a recording stops.
- The meeting page shows the transcript: click a time to hear it, rename or merge voices,
  correct, split or delete lines, then Validate. Ctrl+K finds meetings and transcript
  lines.
- Audio is deleted 10 days (setting) after the transcript is validated, or made when it
  never is; the meeting page warns two days before. The transcript stays.

## Compatibility

- Database schema 16 (transcripts): update desktops and the sync server together.
- Server image: `ghcr.io/alexanderjacquot/task-master-server:v0.23.1`.
