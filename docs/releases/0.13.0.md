# task-master 0.13.0

## Calendar

- **Plan a day or a week.** A new **Plan** button in the day and week views opens a
  planning bar. Pick a focus (a project or the Inbox, an epic, a status) and your working
  hours (9 AM to 5 PM by default, weekends optional), then press **Suggest**: the free
  time is filled with the focus's open tasks, shown as dashed blocks.
  - Urgent, in-progress, overdue and due-soon tasks are more likely to come first; a
    task's prerequisites always come before it; a task due during the shown days lands
    on or before its due date.
  - It plans around your existing blocks and your Google Calendar events, uses each
    task's estimate (one hour without one), and splits a long task when no free slot
    holds it.
  - A light week is spread over its days rather than packed into Monday.
  - **Reshuffle** draws another plan, the × on a suggestion leaves it out, **Approve**
    puts them all on the calendar, and one undo takes the whole plan back off.
  - The task list beside the calendar shows only the focus's tasks while planning. The
    focus and hours are remembered on this computer.

## Board

- **Drag cards to the sidebar.** Drop a card on a project to move the task there, on an
  epic to set its epic, or on Today to put it on today's list.
- **Drag cards to the open task.** Drop a card on the task detail panel to make it a
  subtask of that task, or on the strip at the top to make it the parent. Each drop is one
  undo; drops that would change nothing or make a loop are not offered.
- **Idle swimlanes start folded.** A lane whose cards are all done or cancelled starts
  collapsed, and opens again when a card in it is reopened or one is dropped in. A lane you
  open or fold by hand keeps your choice.

## Lineage

- **Show subtasks** in the filter bar (on by default): turn it off to hide subtasks, and
  their links, from the canvas and the "Not on the canvas" list. Remembered with the other
  Lineage filters.

## Everywhere

- **Type to jump in menus.** In an open dropdown, typing jumps to the first option that
  starts with what you typed, or else contains it.
- **More epic shapes:** bookmark, pin, tag, branch, lineage and hourglass.

## Server and upgrade

No database changes: schema stays at **12**, so 0.8.0 to 0.13.0 desktops and servers sync
with each other. The server image `ghcr.io/alexanderjacquot/task-master-server:v0.13.0`
is published as usual; upgrading the server is optional.

Installed desktops can update from **Settings → About → Check for updates**.
New installations use the Windows setup executable, macOS DMG, or Linux
AppImage/DEB/RPM. Updates are signed; installers are not platform code-signed.
