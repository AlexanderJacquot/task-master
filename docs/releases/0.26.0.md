# task-master 0.26.0

A Roadmap to plan epics and their tasks on a timeline, calendar sync with classic Outlook on
Windows, a Today panel next to All tasks, and smaller conveniences around finishing tasks,
timers and quick capture.

## Roadmap

- **A Roadmap page** in the sidebar, under Lineage. It shows your **epics across projects**
  on a timeline (days, weeks, months or quarters), grouped by project. Each bar ends on the
  epic's target date and fills with the share of its tasks that are done. Arrows show which
  epic waits on which, read from the links between their tasks; an epic that starts before
  one it waits on ends is flagged.
- **Each epic's timeline**: open an epic from the Roadmap, or with **Timeline** on the epic's
  page, to line up its tasks (subtasks under their parent). A task's bar ends on its due
  date; the epic's target date is a dashed line you can drag. Tasks that end after it, or
  start before a prerequisite ends, are flagged.
- **Planning by dragging**: move a bar, drag either end, or drag across an empty row to
  schedule an epic or task (its Schedule button does the same from today). The end changes
  the real target or due date; where a bar starts is kept by the roadmap. From the
  keyboard: ←/→ move a bar, Alt+←/→ its start, Ctrl/⌘+←/→ its end.
- **Saved roadmaps**: "Save as view" keeps the projects, the timescale and where bars start,
  for the roadmap or one epic's timeline, under Saved views.

## Calendar

- **Classic Outlook on Windows**: Settings → Calendar → Connect Outlook syncs with the
  calendar of the Outlook installed on your PC, with no account sign-in or admin rights.
  Time blocks, plans and deadlines go into your main calendar (private; all-day items
  free), and your meetings show in task-master; **Open in Outlook** on an event. One
  calendar account at a time, Google or Outlook, on this computer only. Classic Outlook
  must be installed: the new Outlook app has no interface for this. Events land in your
  Exchange calendar, so they also show in the new Outlook, on the web and on phones.

## Tasks and lists

- **A Today panel on All tasks**: the **Today** button in the header opens today's tasks on
  the right. Drag tasks onto it to plan them for today, remove them, or check them off.
- **List rows drag**, alone or as a selection, onto the Today panel, the sidebar's Today,
  a project or an epic.
- **Marking a task done closes it**: its side panel closes, or its page goes back to its
  project. Turn it off in Settings → General → Finishing tasks.
- **A running timer stops** when its task is marked done or cancelled, from anywhere, and
  the time is logged.
- **Board swimlanes with no open tasks are hidden**, such as a finished epic's. Display →
  "Show lanes with no open tasks" brings them back.

## Quick capture and agents

- **Start the timer from quick capture**: Alt+Enter, or the Start timer button, saves the
  task and starts its timer.
- **Fix (macOS):** quick capture opens with the cursor in the field, and Shift+Enter keeps
  it in the description.
- **Send to agent**: Cmd+Enter (Ctrl+Enter on Windows and Linux) starts the session.

## For contributors

- Linux and WSL builds no longer need libclang: devenv uses whisper's shipped bindings
  there.
- ADR 030 (Outlook calendar) and ADR 031 (Roadmap).

## Compatibility

- Database schema 17, as in 0.24.0 and 0.25.0: no migration, and a 0.24 or 0.25 sync
  server keeps working. Saved roadmap views open as plain "All tasks" lists in older apps,
  and an older app that saves such a view drops its roadmap settings.
- Server image: `ghcr.io/alexanderjacquot/task-master-server:v0.26.0`.
