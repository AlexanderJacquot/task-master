# task-master 0.20.1

## App icon

- New app icon: a eucalyptus zen stone stack. It is the window, taskbar and dock icon,
  the installers' icon, and the in-app logo (sidebar, collapsed rail, About and the
  favicon). The clean theme keeps its own mark.
- It is also an **App logo** option in the asset lab (Ctrl+Alt+A, development builds).
- Installed apps show the new native icon after updating; on Windows, the taskbar may
  keep the old one until Explorer refreshes its icon cache.

## Compatibility

- No database change (schema 14): syncs with 0.17.1 and later desktops and servers.
- Server image: `ghcr.io/alexanderjacquot/task-master-server:v0.20.1` (no server change).
