# task-master 0.28.0

Team spaces: a shared space for a team, next to your personal one, with assignees, comments
and mentions. Also roadmap images (from the app or from the sync server), taking a bar off
the roadmap, double-click to open a bar, and Notes higher up in the sidebar.

## Team spaces (first version)

Only for a sync server started with `TM_TEAM=on` (docs/server.md, section 7a; ADR 033).
Without it, nothing changes: a personal server, or no server, shows nothing of teams.

- **A Team section in the sidebar** with the team's projects and notes, and a **Team
  overview** page. The team's database syncs alongside your personal one; each has its own
  history, and Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes in both.
- **Share to team**: move a personal project, with its tasks, into the team space.
- **Assignee and reporter** on team tasks; every change records who made it.
- **Comments** on team tasks, with **@mentions**. Mentions and assignments show under
  **For you** on the Team overview, with a count in the sidebar.
- **Your Today stays yours**: picking a team task for Today, or planning it in your
  calendar, stays in your personal space.
- **Server**: users and tokens bound to a user (`user add|list|rename|remove`, `token add
  --user`), a personal space for each member, `/team/…` routes, and backups of every
  space. A removed member's tokens are revoked, and their app deletes its copy of the team
  space.

## Roadmap

- **Export image** in the Roadmap's header (the roadmap of epics, an epic's timeline, saved
  roadmap views): Copy as PNG, Save as PNG…, Save as SVG…, in the light, dark or clean
  theme. Only scheduled bars are drawn.
- **Roadmap images from the sync server**: `GET /roadmap.svg` and `GET /roadmap.png` for a
  saved view or an epic, with an `api:read` or `sync` token, to put a roadmap in a wiki or
  a dashboard (docs/server.md, "Roadmap images"; ADR 035).
- **Take a bar off the roadmap**: from its row's right-click menu, with Delete or Backspace
  on the bar, or with the × beside its name. This clears its target or due date
  (Ctrl/⌘+Z brings it back).
- **Double-click a bar to open it**: an epic's timeline, or the task. Settings → General →
  Roadmap turns this off.
- Ctrl+click on a bar no longer starts a drag (on macOS it is a right-click).

## Notes

- **Notes moved up** into the top list of the sidebar, between Lineage and Roadmap. **New
  folder** now sits next to **New note** in its header.
- Headings in the note editor are sized as they are when rendered.

## macOS

- Macs updated from 0.26 or older (still installed as `task-master.app`) now show **Task
  Master** in the Dock and Finder too. The folder keeps its name, so an MCP path set up by
  hand keeps working.

## For contributors

- ADR 033 (team spaces), ADR 034 (roadmap images), ADR 035 (roadmap images from the
  server; the per-view share tokens it proposes are not built). Migrations 20 (spaces), 21
  (identity: op authors, assignee, reporter, members) and 22 (comments, notices).
- The server crate adds `resvg` 0.50 (`text` feature only), with Inter embedded as the
  only font of server-drawn PNGs.

## Compatibility

- **Database schema 22** (0.27 was at 19): **update your sync server together with your
  desktops**. A desktop syncs only with a server at the same schema. Your data is migrated
  on first start; keep a backup if you want to be able to go back to 0.27.
- Server image: `ghcr.io/alexanderjacquot/task-master-server:v0.28.0`.
