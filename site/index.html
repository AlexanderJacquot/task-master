Your tasks and calendar, together.

Plan tasks, organize projects, and make time for the work ahead. Your data starts on your device, with optional Google Calendar integration and sync through a server you choose.

Desktop app for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Calendar connection is optional.

Plan your work

Capture tasks, group them into projects and epics, add notes and files, and connect prerequisites on a canvas.

Work with a local database. Enable a self-hosted sync server or remote backups when you need them.

Optional integration

Connect Google Calendar

With your permission, task-master creates a dedicated task-master calendar for scheduled tasks, day plans, and due dates. Changes to those events in Google Calendar can update the matching schedule or due date in task-master.

You can also choose other Google calendars to display in the app. Those calendars are read-only in task-master.

Create and manage app calendars
Keep task-master events in a calendar created by the app.
Read your calendar list and events
Let you choose calendars and see their events while planning tasks.

Google sign-in opens in your browser. Calendar runs on your desktop unless you explicitly move it to your sync server.
